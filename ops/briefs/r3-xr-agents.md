# Assignment r3: AI agents in XR and in 3D environments; the closest prior work and the gap

Output file: `docs/research/3d-gui-agent/03-agents-in-xr-and-3d.md`

## Goal

Find everything that comes close to a "3D GUI agent", so that the column can state precisely what exists,
what the idea adds, and where it overlaps with others. This dossier matters for priority: be exhaustive and
exact about overlap, and search the newest work (2025-2026 conferences and arXiv up to November 2026) with
several query formulations (for example "GUI agent VR", "XR agent", "spatial computing agent", "LLM agent
virtual reality interface", "AR assistant operate interface", "3D UI grounding", "VR app automation LLM",
"agent visionOS", "Android XR agent", "embodied GUI agent").

## Cover at least

1. **LLMs and VLMs inside VR, AR and MR:** systems that build or change virtual worlds from language (for
   example LLMR, CHI 2024), XR assistants and copilots, gaze- or pointing-aware AR assistants (for example
   GazePointAR), conversational agents in VR, LLM-based XR authoring and programming, and newer work at CHI,
   UIST, ISMAR, IEEE VR, VRST, IUI and arXiv.
2. **Agents that operate XR applications:** automated testing and exploration of VR / AR apps (search-based,
   RL-based and LLM-based), agents that navigate or play in VR, any work on agents that operate visionOS,
   Android XR, Meta Quest or WebXR interfaces, and voice assistants that can act on headset UIs.
3. **Agents acting in 3D through screens or controls:** game agents (SIMA and SIMA 2, Cradle, Voyager and
   Minecraft agents, agents for 3D games and simulators), embodied web agents, and what they show about
   perception and action in 3D through a rendered view.
4. **Assistants on smart glasses and egocentric AI:** Meta AI on Ray-Ban Meta glasses and Orion, Google
   Project Astra, Gemini on Android XR, Apple Intelligence on visionOS, and egocentric benchmarks and models
   (Ego4D, Ego-Exo4D, EgoSchema, EgoLife, and newer): what they perceive, and whether they act on interfaces.
5. **3D and spatial understanding for agents:** 3D-LLMs, spatial VLMs (SpatialVLM, SpatialRGPT and newer),
   scene graphs, 3D grounding of objects from language, and any work on grounding UI elements in 3D space.
6. **Datasets of XR interfaces and interactions:** screenshots, recordings or traces of VR / AR app use, XR UI
   element datasets, and gaze or hand interaction datasets that an agent could learn from.
7. **Human-AI interaction in XR:** shared control, explanations in space, visual guidance (arrows,
   highlights, ghost hands), trust, and proactive assistance.

## Depth

- Key-works table: at least 70 works.

  (none, partial, substantial) explaining exactly what overlaps.
- Close with "The gap": what no existing work does yet, stated as concretely as the evidence allows, and the
  closest five works ranked by overlap.
